Meet our lab director

Yong-Ki Lee

Lab Director

🇩🇪 🇺🇸 🇰🇷

Assistant Professor at Graduate school of Design, Dongseo University. I received my Ph.D. Degree in Industrial Design from KAIST, South Korea. With more than 10 years of teaching experience, my goal is to produce service design graduates who can make an impact. My four main research areas are UX Design, Service Design, Social Innovation, and Sustainable Development.
